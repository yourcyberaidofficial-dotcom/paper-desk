# Product specification

Aureon Trading Lab is a research and paper-trading intelligence desk for BTC, ETH, and gold.

Intended capabilities:
- Live market desk with timestamped data and freshness.
- Explainable strategy council (E1, SMA20, RSI14).
- Research laboratory with promotion gates.
- Durable user accounts and preferences.
- Per-user Telegram linking and daily digests (not yet implemented).
- Risk settings and paper trading where implemented.

Current constraints:
- Production strategy is WAIT_DEFAULT.
- Broker execution is disabled.
- No subscription or billing layer.
- No strategy has passed promotion gates.
- Experimental observations must be labeled as not validated.
